The strategy

Three gates every decision must clear.

Gate 1 · Value

It pays.

Every dollar of AI spend is offset by verified labor-hour savings — measured after deployment, never assumed on a slide.

Gate 2 · Mechanism

Designed against the frontier expected at launch, 6–12 months out — which also reveals what not to build.

Gate 3 · Consensus

Fear → acceptance → embrace, each person at their own pace. Grown through hands-on exposure, never forced.
