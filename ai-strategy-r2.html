策略报告

这版用于团队内部沟通：先讲为什么必须做 AI，再讲 AI 能力从“提供信息”到“回答问题、采取行动、解决问题、自我优化”的阶段判断，最后落到我们未来 6 个月、3 年和 5 年的行动路线。

主线判断

  • 1AI 的本质是能力拓展：把原来能做的事做得更快，也让人开始做原来做不到的事。

AI Transformation
Strategy

This is the single source page for today's AI strategy material. It consolidates the full working logic: why AI matters, where AI capability is today, what data foundation is required, how to govern adoption, how to measure value, and how to turn the material into an executive deck.

Version: R2 bilingual source Scope: digital AI first Role: source page → executive deck Updated: 2026-06-06

Main Position

  • 1AI expands human capability: it makes existing work faster and enables work that was previously difficult or impossible.
  • 2AI should be framed as a partner, not a replacement. It gives people additional reach, speed, and leverage.
  • 3Today AI is strongest at answering questions and is beginning to take action in digital workflows.
  • 4Physical-world manufacturing and R&D AI still require stronger data, validation, governance, and human approval.

01
Why AI

AI 的最高层价值，是扩展人的能力边界。

如果从更高层面看，AI 主要干两件事：第一，把我们原来就能做的事情做得更快；第二，让我们开始做一些原来做不了的事情。这两件事共同指向一个结论：AI 不是人的替代品，而是人的能力放大器。

Acceleration

把已有工作做快

搜索、总结、写代码、准备报告、整理会议、分析资料，把原来需要几小时或几天的数字工作压缩到分钟级。

Expansion

打开原来做不到的事

一个人可以同时拥有研究员、程序员、编辑、数据分析师和项目助理的部分能力，开始跨越原来的专业边界。

更准确的比喻不是“AI 替代人”，而是“AI 成为人的左膀右臂”：像给一个人装上翅膀，让他可以飞。
在互联网时代，工具帮助我们找到信息；在 AI 时代，工具开始帮助我们形成答案、采取数字行动，并逐渐进入问题解决。

信息太多

邮件、会议、文档、报表、工艺记录和历史案例已经分散在多个系统里，单靠人工搜索和记忆会越来越慢。

问题更复杂

先进制程和制造流程的变量高度耦合，单点经验不足以支撑稳定判断，需要系统化提问和跨数据源分析。

窗口正在打开

外部 AI 工具已经证明“回答问题”和“数字行动”可行，大型企业内部仍处于早期，先建立使用能力就是机会。

02
Capability evolution

AI 能力可以看成五个阶段：信息、答案、行动、解决问题、自我优化。

今天的现实判断要谨慎：数字世界里，AI 已经可以稳定回答问题，并开始在代码、文档、邮件、网页、数据整理等场景采取行动；但它还没有普遍达到完整解决问题，更没有在物理世界可靠自主闭环。

Internet era

提供信息

搜索引擎、数据库、Wiki 和报表把信息交给人，判断和行动主要由人完成。

Now

回答问题

LLM、RAG 和企业 Copilot 能把分散信息整合成答案，是当前企业最应该先做扎实的阶段。

Emerging

采取行动

Agent 能在数字系统中写代码、整理文档、生成报告、触发流程，但仍需要权限、审计和人工确认。

Next

解决问题

AI 能拆解目标、选择工具、验证结果并迭代方案。这个阶段在少数数字场景出现，企业内仍需建设。

AGI direction

自我优化

AI 能从成功和失败中持续提高自己的方法论，并参与研发优化。这更接近长期愿景。

03
Current position

我们的起点不是零，但仍处在“回答问题”的早期。

内部可以使用部分企业级 AI 能力，尤其是 M365 生态中的邮件、会议、文档和协同数据。这已经足以支撑很多 AI Agent 的早期功能。但受数据安全、模型能力和工厂底层数据接入限制影响，我们距离“AI 采取行动”和“AI 解决问题”还有明显距离。

6-12M

保守估计：大型企业内部应用能力通常落后外部 AI 工具前沿约 6 到 12 个月。差距不是坏消息，因为行业都在早期，这正是追赶窗口。

使用率 目前更多人只是偶尔用 AI 回答问题，提问数量和问题质量都还没有形成团队级习惯。
工具限制 外部顶级编程模型和 agent 工具能力很强，但在内部环境中不能直接充分使用，必须接受企业安全边界。
内部能力 内部模型和平台可以作为起点，但能力、工具链和上下文接入还需要持续增强。
数据边界 AI 目前主要接触工程外围信息，尚未直接、系统、安全地接入工厂底层关键数据。
机会点 M365 可访问的信息已经很多，可以先用来做会议、文档、邮件、知识提取、报告生成和协作 agent。
04
Data foundation

AI 要真正有用，先要让它访问数据、连接数据，并知道唯一正确的数字。

从信息史看，AI 不是突然出现的孤立工具，而是信息入口的一次升级：从少数人能读一本书，到百科全书、图书馆、互联网搜索，再到 AI 把信息连接起来并直接生成答案。放到公司内部，很多数据仍像分散在不同图书馆里的书。AI 可以成为统一入口，但前提是数据可访问、可连接、可信、可追溯。

Literacy

少数人读书

信息入口掌握在少数能读写的人手里，能读一本书就是能力差异。

Books

百科全书

信息被系统记录，但入口仍有限，知识集中在少数书和少数人手里。

Library

图书馆

大量信息集中起来，但检索成本很高，需要跑很多地方、翻很多资料。

Internet

互联网搜索

全球数字信息被打通，搜索引擎能给相关材料，但不直接给最终判断。

AI

连接并回答

AI 不只是找信息，而是把信息之间的关系处理后，面向问题给出答案。

公司里的数据像多个未打通的图书馆

很多工程数据、spec、process flow、实验记录和历史问题都存在，但分散在不同系统里。这个问题在互联网时代就应该被解决；到了 AI 时代，它变成 AI 能否发挥作用的基础条件。

  • 第一步不是让 AI “自由发挥”，而是先让关键数据数字化、可读取、可搜索。
  • 第二步是把不同数据源之间的关系打通，让 AI 能跨系统理解上下文。
  • 第三步才是让 AI 成为接口，帮助工程师搜索、汇总、关联、解释和发现异常。

半导体行业必须同时“开放”和“收敛”

AI 需要能访问海量信息，但在 process flow、工艺 spec、ET spec 等场景，它不能给模糊答案。工程数字差 0.1 nm 都可能改变判断，因此必须有 single source of truth。

  • 同一时刻，一个关键 spec 应该只有一个权威参考源。
  • AI 的答案必须引用这个权威源，而不是从多个冲突系统中平均或猜测。
  • SSOT 不只是 IT 项目，而是让 AI 进入严肃工程判断的前提。
可访问AI 能读取数据，而不是只能读外围文档。
可连接数据源之间有关系、上下文和统一 ID。
唯一正确关键 spec 和 flow 有唯一权威来源。
可追溯每个答案能回到数据源、版本和时间点。
对工程师最直接的起点，仍然是每天最简单的痛点：搜索、汇总、对比、找相关性。数据打通和 SSOT 做好以后，AI 才能发现人平时看不到的关联。
05
Roadmap

路线图应该分三段：6-12 个月做好回答问题，3 年进入解决问题，5 年参与部分研发提高。

不要一开始就追求“全自动 AI 工厂”。更务实的顺序是：先让团队真的会问、会用、会验证；再让 AI 在数字工作流里行动；最后接入关键数据和验证机制，让它在受控范围内解决工程问题。

0-6 个月

目标：把回答问题做好

建立团队级 prompt 模板、问题库、案例库和 M365 Copilot 使用流程，让每个人都能把 AI 用在日常分析、会议准备、文档总结和代码辅助上。

验收：使用率和答案质量

看周活跃用户、有效问题数量、节省时间、报告质量、答案引用可信度，而不是只看部署了几个工具。

6-12 个月

目标：从答案走向数字行动

让 AI 开始自动整理会议行动项、生成 follow-up、准备 review 包、更新知识库、跑固定分析脚本和建立轻量 agent workflow。

验收：可审计的行动闭环

每个 action 都有触发条件、数据来源、人工确认和日志记录。先做低风险数字任务，不碰高风险物理控制。

1-3 年

目标：解决一类重复工程问题

围绕良率异常、defect pattern、process drift、review preparation 等场景，把数据、历史案例、推理和验证流程串起来。

验收：从建议到验证

AI 不只是给解释，而是提出候选根因、推荐检查顺序、调用分析工具，并通过历史对比或小规模验证降低不确定性。

3-5 年

目标：参与部分研发和优化

在受控范围内让 AI 参与 DOE 设计、工艺窗口分析、参数敏感性研究、知识沉淀和 R&D 到 HVM 转移。

验收：人机共同提高

成功案例被系统记录，失败案例进入反例库，AI 和团队都能从反馈中改进方法。

06
Operating plan

部门层面的第一步，是把 AI 从个人兴趣变成团队能力。

如果只靠少数人自发尝试，组织不会真正转型。需要明确场景、工具边界、数据规则、使用习惯和度量指标，让 AI 使用成为工程流程的一部分。

建立“AI 问题库”

收集团队高频问题、领导常问问题、优秀工程师的分析路径。重点不是保存答案，而是保存好的提问方式和判断框架。

建立“可用场景清单”

先覆盖低风险高频任务：会议总结、邮件归纳、代码解释、debug 思路、报告草稿、数据图表说明、review checklist。

建立“安全边界”

明确哪些数据可以进入企业 AI，哪些只能本地处理，哪些场景必须人工确认。安全规则越清晰，使用越容易推广。

建立“行动型 Agent 试点”

选 2-3 个数字工作流，让 AI 从回答走向行动，例如自动准备会议包、自动整理 follow-up、自动更新知识库。

1-2每周随周报推 1-2 条小 tips，让大家能直接照着试。
3-5先跟踪 3-5 个高频工作流，观察 AI 是否真正进入流程。
Case记录真实案例：原来多久、现在多久、答案是否带来源。
Quality看是否被采用、是否减少返工、是否提升准备质量。
衡量要“轻”：不要把“50% 的工作必须用 AI 完成”、prompt 数、token 数或固定使用时长写成个人 KPI。那会让人为了指标而用，甚至产生逆反。更好的做法是一边轻推，一边看具体场景有没有被 AI 改善。
不要量token、prompt 数、每天必须用多久、百分之多少工作必须用 AI。
可以量哪些任务被 AI 覆盖、哪些答案带来源、哪些会议准备变快。
怎么推每周周报放 1-2 个一句话 tips，低门槛、可复制、不说教。
怎么拉让团队看到真实节省时间的例子，自然形成“先问 AI”的习惯。

下一步可以补充的内容

  1. 加入台积电、三星、NVIDIA、EDA/设备厂商在 AI 制造和 AI 研发上的公开案例。
  2. 把“我们有什么工具、市场有什么工具、哪些不能用、哪些能安全用”整理成一页工具矩阵。
  3. 把报告压成真正的 4-5 页 PPT：每页一个核心句、三条支撑、一个行动建议。

07
Governance & pilots

真正的难点不是做出 agent，而是让它在组织里可信、可控、有人负责。

外部调研和企业案例给出的信号很一致：AI 项目失败通常不是因为模型不会回答，而是因为数据散、流程没改、权责不清、人有恐惧。对大公司尤其如此。我们的打法应该避开政治硬墙，在已经授权的数据边界内做小步试点，用带来源、可追溯、可拒答的 agent 建立信任。

Enterprise AI reality

失败多卡在集成和规模化

MIT NANDA 2025 报告被广泛引用的结论是，多数企业 GenAI pilot 没有产生可衡量 P&L 影响；核心问题更多是 workflow integration 和落地学习差距，而非模型本身。source

People / process / politics

AI 战略也是组织设计

HBS AI Institute 对 HBR 文章的摘要强调，AI ROI 取决于 people、processes、politics，需要处理员工恐惧、重建流程和管理权力变化。source

Approved boundary

M365 是最现实的合规入口

Microsoft 文档说明 Copilot 可通过 Microsoft Graph 基于用户权限访问邮件、文件、聊天、会议等组织数据，并提供 citations 供核验。source

政治护城河：先守住公司已经批准的 M365 / Copilot 边界，不申请新权限，不碰生产控制，不替代人的判断，只把本来能找到的信息更快、更可靠地交给工程师。
1

Knowledge Check Agent

最该先做的 flagship use case。它访问的是已经授权的文档、邮件、training material、meeting recap 和 share drive 信息，本质是帮大家更快找到本来就能找到的东西，政治阻力低，价值直接。

硬规则：每个回答必须带来源；答不出来就说“不知道”；宁可拒答，也不能编。
2

Meeting Prep Coach

把“Program Manager 数字分身”重新叙事为开会准备助手。它服务的是汇报人，帮助工程师预演会被问到的硬问题、提前补齐证据，而不是替代 PM 的判断和权力。

政治动作：先做通用版；如果涉及某位 PM 的风格，让 PM 成为共建者，而不是被建模对象。
3

Domain Knowledge Ownership

不建议每个工程师配 5 个 agent。更稳的结构是部门维护、按领域收敛：知识库有 owner，信息准确性有人负责，agent 只是检索和搬运接口。

治理原则：事实层收敛，应用层发散；spec、数据、SOP 必须唯一准确，怎么问和怎么用可以多样。
Yield / MTS inline specOwner: Integration。对 spec 定义、版本、例外情况和引用路径负责。
Reliability 数据Owner: Reliability team。对可靠性数据口径、测试条件和历史案例负责。
Logistics / planningOwner: Planning / Manufacturing。对排程、产能、物流状态和变更记录负责。
Trust带来源、可追溯、允许说不知道。
Boundary只用已授权数据，不越权访问。
Ownership每个知识库有人负责准确性。
KPI用任务覆盖率和 30 秒带来源答案，而不是 prompt 数。

中肯排序

  1. 先做 Knowledge Check Agent，选 1-2 个高痛点技术域试点，强制带来源和拒答机制。
  2. 把数字分身改成 Meeting Prep Coach，先做通用版，再考虑让关键 PM 共建。
  3. 建立 knowledge ownership，比 agent 本身更重要：谁负责哪个库，谁对准确性负责。
  4. 汇报 KPI 讲任务覆盖和时间缩短，例如“80% 常见技术问题 30 秒内拿到带来源答案”。

这一页是工作草稿：外部研究只作为方向性依据，最终内部报告需要替换成公司可引用的正式来源和合规表述。

08
Workstyle evolution

不要先讲“组织变革”，先把它做成没人反对的工作方式改善。

更长远看，AI 会改变部门之间对齐、会议、系统设计和知识流动的方式。但在真正做出可见成果前，最好不要把它包装成“组织架构调整”。更安全的说法是：减少同步会议成本、提升会前异步对齐质量、让人把时间用在判断和决策上。

Agent orchestration

人定方向，agent 做例行协调

微软已把 agents、agent builder、workflows 和 Teams/M365 集成作为企业 Copilot 方向之一。现实落点不是无人组织，而是人做判断，agent 处理数据、状态汇总和例行流程。source

MCP + A2A

纵向接数据，横向接 agent

MCP 更像 agent 到工具、API、数据库的连接层；A2A 则提供 agent-to-agent 的通信和协作协议。你的“部门 agent 接自己数据库，再和别部门 agent 对齐”正好对应这两层。MCP · A2A

Human oversight

信息可以自动对齐，责任不能自动消失

企业 agent 治理正在从每步人工审批，走向人设定边界、阈值和升级路径。agent 可以交换信息，但决策问责必须落在人头上。source

战略主轴：变革要做成“被发现的既成事实”，不要做成“被宣布的计划”。先让一个会变短、一个状态对齐变快、一个常见问题能 30 秒带来源回答。

会议从同步状态汇报，变成异步对齐后的判断会

今天很多会议昂贵但低效：大量时间花在每个人重复听并不相关的状态。更现实的第一步，是会前让 knowledge agent 汇总各方状态、风险、open questions 和需要决策的点。会议本身只保留判断、冲突解决和关键决策。

  • 会前：agent 汇总状态和证据，发出 pre-read。
  • 会中：人只讨论无法自动解决的判断问题。
  • 会后：agent 生成 action items、owner、deadline 和 follow-up。

系统未来要同时服务人和 AI，但现在不要这么讲

长期趋势是系统不只提供给人看的 UI，也要提供给 AI 可读取、可调用、可审计的接口。这个方向是真实的，但对上汇报时不宜讲成“系统要为 AI 重新设计”，而应讲成“让数据能被授权 agent 安全访问、引用和追溯”。

  • 对人：界面清楚、流程可执行。
  • 对 AI：API、权限、版本、provenance、事件和日志清楚。
  • 对组织：不越权、不黑箱、可追责。

可以做，但不要命名太大异步对齐、跨部门 agent 辅助协调、meeting prep coach、减少 status meeting 时间。

对上汇报的安全话术

  1. “我们先把例行状态会的会前准备自动化，让会议时间更多用于判断和决策。”
  2. “我们先在一个技术域试点，把常见问题变成 30 秒内可拿到、带来源的答案。”
  3. “我们不是改变组织架构，而是在现有权限和流程内减少重复沟通成本。”
  4. “agent 可以对齐信息，但最终判断、批准和责任仍在人。”

这一页保留为个人 roadmap 草稿：对外汇报时应压缩成“会议成本 + knowledge agent + 数据安全访问”三件事，其他内容先作为长期方向。

Narrative compression

最容易讲清楚的方法：同一件事，昨天怎么做、今天怎么做、明天怎么做。

这页是把前面的思考压成能对团队讲的版本。不要从抽象概念开始，而是从每天都经历的事情开始：定会议、开会被追问数据、周报同步。听众先看到自己的痛点，再看到 AI 如何把工作从“我去做”变成“我确认”，最后变成“信息自己流到我面前”。

人的价值：AI 能传递信息，但建立不了信任。

客户愿意合作，不只是因为这次数据好，而是因为他相信下次数据不好时你也会拼命做好。这种“即使数据不好也愿意在一起”的关系，是人和人之间建立的，AI 给不了。AI 处理信息，人承担判断、关系和信任。

01
Why AI

The highest-level value of AI is expanding human capability.

AI does two things for us: it makes work we already do faster, and it lets us do work we could not realistically do before. This is the core framing: AI is not the replacement of people; it is a capability multiplier for people and teams.

Acceleration

Make existing work faster

Search, summarize, write, prepare reviews, organize meetings, explain data, and compress hours of digital work into minutes.

Expansion

Enable work we could not do before

One engineer can borrow partial capability from a researcher, programmer, editor, data analyst, and project assistant at the same time.

The better metaphor is not "AI replaces people." It is "AI gives people wings": a side-by-side assistant that expands reach, speed, and leverage.

Information overload

Email, meetings, reports, specs, process records, and historical cases are scattered across systems. Manual memory and search cannot scale.

Higher complexity

Advanced manufacturing and process integration involve coupled variables. We need better questioning, synthesis, and cross-source analysis.

Open window

External tools prove that answering questions and taking digital action are now practical. Large enterprises are still early, so adoption speed matters.

02
Capability evolution

AI capability moves through five stages: information, answers, action, problem solving, and self-improvement.

The realistic view today is conservative. In the digital world, AI can already answer questions and is beginning to act in code, documents, email, web pages, and data workflows. It has not yet broadly reached reliable end-to-end problem solving, especially in the physical world.

Internet era

Provide information

Search engines, databases, wikis, and dashboards gave people information. Humans still did most judgment and action.

Now

Answer questions

LLMs, RAG, and enterprise copilots can synthesize scattered information into useful answers. This is the stage we should first make solid.

Emerging

Take action

Agents can write code, organize documents, generate reports, and trigger workflow steps, but they need permission, audit trails, and human confirmation.

Next

Solve problems

AI decomposes goals, chooses tools, verifies outputs, and iterates. This is emerging in selected digital domains, but not yet a mature enterprise norm.

AGI direction

Self-improve

AI learns from success and failure, improves its methods, and contributes to R&D optimization. This remains a longer-term direction.

03
Current position

Our starting point is not zero, but we are still early in the "answering questions" phase.

We already have some enterprise AI capability, especially through the M365 ecosystem: email, meetings, documents, recaps, and collaboration data. That is enough to support many early AI workflows. But security boundaries, model capability, and limited access to factory-level data still constrain action and problem solving.

6-12M

Large enterprises often trail the external AI frontier by roughly six to twelve months. This gap is not only a weakness; it also means there is still a clear adoption window.

UsageMany people still use AI only occasionally. The team has not yet built a habit of asking better questions and validating sourced answers.
Tool limitsExternal coding and agent tools are powerful, but cannot be freely used inside the enterprise boundary.
Internal toolsInternal platforms are a starting point, but model strength, toolchains, and context access still need improvement.
Data boundaryAI mostly touches surrounding engineering information today, not the full factory data foundation.
OpportunityM365-accessible data is already enough for meetings, documents, email, knowledge extraction, and report preparation.
04
Data foundation

For AI to be useful, it needs access to data, connections across data, and one trusted source of truth.

Information access has evolved from books to libraries, from libraries to internet search, and now from search to AI-generated answers. Inside a company, many datasets still behave like separate libraries. AI can become the unified interface only when data is readable, connected, trusted, and traceable.

Internal data is still fragmented

Engineering data, specs, process flows, experiment records, and historical issues exist, but they are scattered across systems. This was already a digital transformation issue before AI; now it becomes a prerequisite for useful AI.

  • First: make key data digital, readable, searchable, and permissioned.
  • Second: connect data sources through context, IDs, ownership, and provenance.
  • Third: let AI become the interface for search, synthesis, comparison, explanation, and anomaly discovery.

Semiconductor work needs both breadth and precision

AI should access broad information, but process flows, process specs, ET specs, and engineering numbers must converge to one correct reference. A 0.1 nm difference can change judgment.

  • At any point in time, a key spec should have one authoritative reference.
  • AI answers must cite that source, not average across conflicting systems.
  • SSOT is not only an IT project; it is the foundation for serious engineering judgment with AI.
AccessibleAI can read the right data, not only surrounding documents.
ConnectedSources share context, relationships, ownership, and IDs.
AuthoritativeCritical specs and flows have one trusted source.
TraceableEvery answer can point back to source, version, and timestamp.
05
Roadmap

The roadmap should move in stages: answer questions first, then act digitally, then solve selected problems.

We should not start by promising a fully autonomous AI factory. The practical sequence is to build team usage habits, improve answer quality, introduce auditable digital actions, and only then connect deeper engineering data and validation loops.

0-6 months

Make question answering useful

Build prompts, question libraries, example cases, and M365 Copilot workflows for daily analysis, meeting prep, document summaries, and code support.

Validate usage and answer quality

Look at useful scenarios, sourced answers, reduced preparation time, and adoption stories rather than tool deployment alone.

6-12 months

Move from answers to digital action

Let AI prepare meeting packages, summarize action items, draft follow-ups, update knowledge bases, and run fixed analysis workflows.

Build auditable action loops

Every action needs triggers, data sources, human confirmation, and logs. Start with low-risk digital tasks.

1-3 years

Solve repeated engineering problems

Target yield excursions, defect patterns, process drift, review preparation, and repeated technical questions.

From suggestion to validation

AI should propose likely causes, recommend checks, call analysis tools, and reduce uncertainty through historical comparison or small-scale validation.

3-5 years

Contribute to R&D and optimization

Use AI in DOE planning, process window analysis, parameter sensitivity, knowledge capture, and R&D-to-HVM transfer.

Human and AI improve together

Successes and failures become institutional learning assets. AI and the team both improve through feedback.

06
Operating plan

The first department-level step is turning AI from personal interest into team capability.

If AI stays as a hobby for a few early adopters, the organization will not transform. We need usable scenarios, tool boundaries, data rules, adoption habits, and lightweight measurement.

Build an AI question library

Capture frequent team questions, leadership questions, and analysis paths used by strong engineers. The goal is to save good questioning patterns, not just answers.

Build a usable scenario list

Start with low-risk, high-frequency work: meeting summaries, email synthesis, code explanation, debug thinking, report drafts, chart explanations, and review checklists.

Define safety boundaries

Clarify which data can enter enterprise AI, which data stays local, and which actions require human confirmation.

Start action-agent pilots

Pick two or three digital workflows where AI can move from answers to actions, such as meeting prep, follow-up generation, and knowledge updates.

1-2Share one or two practical AI tips in the weekly update so people can copy and try them.
3-5Track three to five common workflows and see whether AI actually enters the workflow.
CaseRecord real examples: before time, after time, and whether the answer had sources.
QualityMeasure whether work is adopted, whether rework is reduced, and whether preparation quality improves.
Measurement should be light. Do not make "50% of work must use AI," token count, prompt count, or fixed daily AI time into personal KPIs. That creates resistance and metric gaming. Measure whether specific work improves.
Do not measuretokens, prompts, mandatory daily time, or percent of work forced into AI.
Do measuretask coverage, sourced answers, meeting prep time, and quality improvement.
Push gentlyweekly one-line tips that are easy to copy.

07
Governance & pilots

The hard part is not building an agent; it is making the agent trusted, governed, and owned.

Enterprise AI failures are often not model failures. They come from fragmented data, unclear process ownership, politics, and fear of replacement. The safe path is to start inside approved M365/Copilot boundaries and build trust through sourced, traceable, refusal-capable answers.

1

Knowledge Check Agent

The flagship low-risk use case. It searches authorized documents, email, training material, meeting recaps, and shared files to help people find what they could already access.

Rule: every answer needs sources. If it does not know, it should say so.
2

Meeting Prep Coach

Reframe the "digital double" idea as a meeting preparation coach. It helps engineers anticipate hard questions and prepare evidence; it does not replace the PM's judgment.

Political move: start with a generic version, then let key stakeholders co-build it.
3

Domain Knowledge Ownership

Avoid giving every engineer many personal agents. Instead, departments own domain knowledge bases, and agents become retrieval and workflow interfaces.

Principle: facts converge; applications can diverge. Specs, SOPs, and data definitions need owners.
Trustsourced, traceable, allowed to say "I do not know."
Boundaryonly use authorized data.
Ownershipeach knowledge base has a responsible owner.
KPIuse task coverage and sourced-answer speed, not prompt count.
08
Workstyle evolution

Do not lead with "organization change." Make it a gradual improvement in how work gets done.

AI will eventually change meetings, alignment, system design, and knowledge flow. But before visible wins exist, it is safer to talk about reducing meeting cost, improving pre-read quality, and letting people spend more time on judgment and decisions.

Meetings become decision forums

Many meetings spend too much time on status. A knowledge agent can summarize status, risks, open questions, and decisions before the meeting. The meeting itself should focus on judgment, conflict resolution, and decisions.

  • Before: agent prepares status and evidence.
  • During: people discuss judgment problems.
  • After: agent drafts action items, owners, due dates, and follow-ups.

Systems should eventually serve people and AI

Long term, systems need not only human UI but also AI-readable, callable, auditable interfaces. For leadership, frame this as safe data access, citation, and traceability, not as redesigning the organization for AI.

  • For people: clear UI and workflow.
  • For AI: APIs, permissions, versions, provenance, events, and logs.
  • For the organization: no privilege bypass and clear accountability.
Saymeeting cost, knowledge search, safe data access.
Avoidreplacement, restructuring, digital doubles negotiating for people.
Prove firstone or two useful workflows.
Keep human rolepeople judge and own decisions; agents accelerate.
09
Narrative compression

The clearest story is: how we did it yesterday, how we do it today, and how it works tomorrow.

Start with daily pain points everyone understands: scheduling meetings, being asked for data in reviews, and preparing weekly updates. This makes the AI story concrete instead of abstract.

Human value: AI can move information, but it cannot build trust.

Customers do not trust us only because this run looks good. They trust us because they believe that when the data is bad, we will still work hard to make it right. AI handles information; people carry judgment, relationships, accountability, and trust.
