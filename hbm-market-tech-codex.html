简报HBM Market & Technology Brief

HBM 正从 AI 训练的高端内存，变成推理吞吐、长上下文和自研 ASIC 的关键瓶颈。2026 年主线是 HBM3E 放量、HBM4 验证与先进封装爬坡；2029-2031 年的 HBM5 则把竞争焦点进一步推向 base die、hybrid bonding 和客户定制。HBM is moving from premium AI training memory to a critical bottleneck for inference throughput, long context workloads, and custom AI ASICs. The 2026 story is HBM3E volume, HBM4 qualification, and advanced packaging ramps; the 2029-2031 HBM5 horizon pushes the battleground toward base die design, hybrid bonding, and customer-specific memory.

01 / Market

市场不是缺 DRAM，而是缺合格 HBM 系统件The market is short qualified HBM systems, not just DRAM bits

HBM 的定价权来自 AI 加速器对本地带宽、容量和功耗的同时要求。传统 DRAM 周期仍重要，但 HBM 已经成为 memory supercycle 中最稀缺的高端切片。HBM pricing power comes from AI accelerators needing local bandwidth, capacity, and power efficiency at the same time. The traditional DRAM cycle still matters, but HBM has become the scarcest high-end slice of the memory supercycle.

02 / Technology

HBM 的技术价值是把带宽贴近计算HBM's technical value is moving bandwidth next to compute

HBM 不是 DDR 的线性升级。它通过 3D 堆叠、TSV、base die 和 2.5D 封装，把 DRAM 放到 GPU/ASIC 旁边，用宽总线换取 TB/s 级带宽和更低每 bit 能耗。HBM is not a linear DDR upgrade. Through 3D stacking, TSVs, a base die, and 2.5D packaging, it puts DRAM beside the GPU/ASIC and trades an ultra-wide bus for TB/s-class bandwidth and lower energy per bit.

03 / HBM5 + Base Die

HBM5 的重点不是更宽总线，而是 base die 控制权HBM5 is not just a wider bus; it is a fight over the base die

目前 HBM4 已有标准和产品公告；HBM5 仍处于路线图和研究窗口。比较稳妥的读法是：2029-2031 年 HBM5/HBM5E/custom HBM5 会把行业从“卖堆叠 DRAM”推向“存储子系统协同设计”。HBM4 is now backed by standards and product announcements; HBM5 remains a roadmap and research horizon. The practical read: in 2029-2031, HBM5/HBM5E/custom HBM5 pushes the industry from selling stacked DRAM toward co-designing memory subsystems.

确认 vs 情景Confirmed vs scenario

如何看 HBM5 资料How to read HBM5 claims

  1. 1已确认：SK hynix 路线图把 HBM5、HBM5E 和 custom HBM5 放在 2029-2031 年窗口。Confirmed: SK hynix's roadmap places HBM5, HBM5E, and custom HBM5 in the 2029-2031 window.
  2. 2高可信趋势：base die 承担更多控制、功耗、测试、遥测和客户接口功能，定制化程度上升。High-confidence trend: the base die takes on more control, power, test, telemetry, and customer-interface functions.
  3. 3情景假设：4096-bit I/O、16/20-hi、约 4TB/s 和更高功耗来自路线图/研究预测，不应当作最终 JEDEC 规格。Scenario: 4096-bit I/O, 16/20-hi, roughly 4TB/s, and higher power are roadmap/research projections, not final JEDEC specs.

Base die 为什么成为核心Why the base die becomes strategic

Base die 是 HBM 堆栈底部的逻辑层，连接 DRAM die、TSV、PHY、测试/修复、电源分配和外部 GPU/ASIC 接口。HBM4 以后，它不再只是“适配层”，而是客户可定制的性能、功耗和封装边界。The base die is the logic layer at the bottom of an HBM stack, linking DRAM dies, TSVs, PHY, test/repair, power distribution, and the external GPU/ASIC interface. After HBM4, it is no longer merely an adapter; it becomes a customer-tunable boundary for performance, power, and packaging.

HBM4 baseline JEDEC HBM4 将 I/O 扩到 2048-bit；厂商产品已把单堆带宽推进到 2.8-3.3TB/s。JEDEC HBM4 expands I/O to 2048-bit; vendor products are already pushing per-stack bandwidth to 2.8-3.3TB/s.
HBM5 scenario 研究路线图常见假设是 4096-bit、16/20-hi 和约 4TB/s，但标准化仍未完成。Research roadmaps often model 4096-bit, 16/20-hi, and roughly 4TB/s, but standardization is not complete.
Hybrid bonding TrendForce 指出 HBM5 20-hi 世代会采用 hybrid bonding，原因是高度、I/O 密度和散热限制。TrendForce expects hybrid bonding for HBM5 20-hi because stack height, I/O density, and thermal limits tighten.

结论：HBM5 的商业价值可能不只在 bit 或带宽，而在谁能控制 base die、堆叠工艺和客户平台验证。Conclusion: HBM5's commercial value may lie less in bits alone and more in who controls the base die, stack process, and customer platform qualification.

标准路径Standard path

内存厂定义 base die，客户按标准 HBM PHY/controller 集成。优点是可替代性更强、导入更稳；缺点是很难针对单一 GPU/ASIC 优化功耗、延迟和封装面积。The memory vendor defines the base die and customers integrate through a standard HBM PHY/controller. This improves substitutability, but limits per-accelerator optimization of power, latency, and package area.

定制路径Custom path

GPU/ASIC 客户与内存厂、代工厂共同定义 base die；控制器、遥测、功耗管理甚至部分近存计算逻辑可下沉。优点是性能/能效更强，代价是验证更长、锁定更深。GPU/ASIC customers co-define the base die with memory and foundry partners; controller, telemetry, power management, and even near-memory logic can move downward. Performance and efficiency improve, but qualification lengthens and lock-in deepens.

代工厂变量Foundry variable

如果 wafer-to-wafer hybrid bonding 要求 base die 与 memory die 尺寸高度匹配，能同时承接 GPU/ASIC 与 base die 的代工/封装平台会更有话语权，TSMC 的角色会继续上升。If wafer-to-wafer hybrid bonding requires tight base-die and memory-die dimension matching, foundry/packaging platforms that handle both GPU/ASIC and base die work gain leverage; TSMC's role likely rises.

04 / Supply chain

三大厂竞争，胜负在量产质量和客户验证The three-way race is decided by production quality and qualification

HBM 的供应链不像普通 DRAM 那样只看 bit output。AI 客户需要稳定的整栈良率、热表现、功耗曲线和封装协同，任何一项掉链都会影响 GPU 或 ASIC 出货。HBM supply is not measured like ordinary DRAM bit output. AI customers need stable full-stack yield, thermal behavior, power curves, and package integration; any weak link can delay GPU or ASIC shipments.

SK hynix

现阶段 HBM 龙头。优势在 NVIDIA 关系、HBM3E 量产、MR-MUF 封装经验和高份额。新变量是 custom HBM：路线图显示其正把控制器功能推向 base die，并与 TSMC 合作 base die。The current HBM leader. Its edge is the NVIDIA relationship, HBM3E volume production, MR-MUF packaging experience, and high share. The new variable is custom HBM: its roadmap points to moving controller functions toward the base die and collaborating with TSMC on base dies.

05 / Watch list

接下来 6-18 个月该看什么What to watch over the next 6-18 months

HBM 的研究重点不只是规格表。真正有预测价值的是客户认证、封装分配、ASP 曲线、非 NVIDIA 需求扩散，以及 HBM5/base die 相关的提前投资信号。The useful signals are not just spec sheets. Customer qualification, package allocation, ASP curves, demand outside NVIDIA, and early HBM5/base-die investment signals carry more predictive power.

Rubin HBM4 验证节奏Rubin HBM4 qualification

NVIDIA Rubin/Rubin Ultra 会决定 HBM4 首轮份额分配。谁先进供应链，谁就更可能在 HBM4E 和 custom HBM 里延续位置。NVIDIA Rubin/Rubin Ultra will set the first HBM4 share allocation. Whoever enters early has a better chance of carrying that position into HBM4E and custom HBM.

16-hi 良率16-hi yield

16-hi 带来更高容量，但热、翘曲和堆叠良率压力更大。它是容量增长的关键，也是供应商分化的地方。16-hi raises capacity, but increases thermal, warpage, and stack-yield pressure. It is key to capacity growth and supplier differentiation.

CoWoS allocation

HBM 有货不等于 GPU 能出货。TSMC 先进封装、interposer 和系统级良率仍会约束 AI 加速器交付。Available HBM does not automatically mean available GPUs. TSMC advanced packaging, interposers, and system-level yield still constrain accelerator delivery.

ASIC attach rate

Google TPU、AWS Trainium、Broadcom/Marvell custom ASIC 的 HBM 用量，会决定市场是否摆脱对 NVIDIA 单一节奏的依赖。HBM usage in Google TPU, AWS Trainium, and Broadcom/Marvell custom ASICs will decide whether the market becomes less dependent on NVIDIA's cadence.

HBM3E ASP

成熟 HBM3E 供给改善后可能承压。若 HBM4 溢价不足，供应商盈利弹性会低于出货增速。Mature HBM3E may face pressure as supply improves. If HBM4 premiums are insufficient, profit leverage may lag shipment growth.

Custom HBM

base die 与客户 ASIC 的协同设计，会把 HBM 从标准品推向平台型组件，长期提高切换成本，也改变内存厂和代工厂的价值分配。Base-die co-design with customer ASICs can move HBM from a standard component toward a platform element, raising switching costs and changing value split between memory vendors and foundries.

HBM5 标准化HBM5 standardization

重点看 4096-bit、16/20-hi、散热预算和封装高度是否进入正式标准或供应商公开规格。标准落地前，所有 HBM5 数字都应按情景处理。Watch whether 4096-bit, 16/20-hi, thermal budgets, and package-height assumptions enter formal standards or public vendor specs. Until then, HBM5 numbers are scenarios.

Hybrid bonding 量产学习曲线Hybrid bonding learning curve

HBM5 20-hi 若采用 wafer-to-wafer hybrid bonding，颗粒控制、前端良率和 base die/memory die 尺寸匹配会成为新瓶颈。If HBM5 20-hi uses wafer-to-wafer hybrid bonding, particle control, front-end yield, and base-die/memory-die size matching become new bottlenecks.
