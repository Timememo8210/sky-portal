FPGA（现场可编程门阵列）是一种可在出厂后由用户配置逻辑功能的半导体器件。与ASIC不同，FPGA无需流片即可实现定制功能，适合小批量、高可靠性、快速迭代的应用场景。全球FPGA市场2025年约$138亿，预计2031年达$257亿。其中国防航天是增速最快的细分市场，2024年约$10.9亿，CAGR 17%，预计2032年突破$32亿。 FPGAs (Field-Programmable Gate Arrays) are semiconductor devices whose logic can be configured by users after manufacturing. Unlike ASICs, FPGAs require no fabrication to implement custom functions, ideal for low-volume, high-reliability, rapid-iteration use cases. The global FPGA market was ~$13.8B in 2025, projected to reach $25.7B by 2031. Defense & Aerospace is the fastest-growing segment with 17% CAGR, from $1.09B in 2024 to $3.2B by 2032.

🔵 Intel / Altera 详解Intel / Altera Deep Dive

Intel于2015年以$167亿收购Altera，但FPGA业务一直未达预期。2024年Altera营收$15.4亿，亏损$6.15亿。2025年Intel将51%股权以$44.6亿出售给Silver Lake，Altera重新独立运营，估值$87.5亿。 Intel acquired Altera for $16.7B in 2015, but the FPGA business underperformed. In 2024, Altera posted $1.54B revenue with a $615M loss. In 2025, Intel sold 51% to Silver Lake for $4.46B, re-establishing Altera as an independent company valued at $8.75B.

Altera的困境：自2013年选择Intel代工后，Altera在制程推进上持续落后于Xilinx/AMD。原因是Intel工厂优先给CPU排产，FPGA排在后面。而TSMC对任何愿意付费的客户开放最新制程。这导致Agilex系列在制程上始终比Versal落后约1代。独立后，Altera是否会转投TSMC是业界最大的悬念之一。 Altera's Dilemma: Since choosing Intel foundry in 2013, Altera has consistently lagged Xilinx/AMD on process nodes — Intel prioritizes CPUs over FPGAs. TSMC, by contrast, offers latest nodes to any paying customer. This put Agilex about one generation behind Versal. Post-independence, whether Altera switches to TSMC is one of the biggest industry questions.

为什么军工偏爱FPGA？ (1) 可重编程：雷达/电子战需要快速适应新威胁波形 (2) 长生命周期：军事平台服役20-30年，FPGA可在原硬件上升级算法 (3) 供应安全：ASIC需要百万级量产才经济，军用量小（通常数千~数万颗），FPGA成本合理 (4) 安全性：Xilinx第4代Defense-Grade安全架构支持加密比特流和防篡改。 Why does defense love FPGAs? (1) Reprogrammable: Radar/EW must quickly adapt to new threat waveforms (2) Long lifecycle: Military platforms serve 20-30 years; FPGAs can upgrade algorithms on existing hardware (3) Supply economics: ASICs need million-unit volumes; military volumes are 1K-10K units — FPGAs are cost-effective (4) Security: Xilinx 4th-gen Defense-Grade architecture supports encrypted bitstreams and tamper resistance.

FPGA为何追不上手机/GPU的制程？ FPGA的可编程逻辑天然面积大、利用率低（典型利用率仅60-80%），晶体管密度无法像GPU那样充分利用先进制程的密度优势。FPGA需要大量I/O和片上RAM，对制程的要求更偏向低功耗、高速I/O、宽金属层，而非纯密度。因此FPGA通常比GPU/CPU落后1-2代制程。 Why do FPGAs lag GPU/CPU on process nodes? FPGA programmable logic inherently wastes area (typical utilization ~60-80%), so transistor density gains from advanced nodes aren't fully leveraged. FPGAs need massive I/O and on-chip RAM, prioritizing low power, high-speed I/O, and wide metal layers over raw density. FPGAs typically trail GPUs/CPUs by 1-2 process generations.

关键技术趋势Key Technology Trends

  • Chiplet化：VP1902已使用4-chiplet架构。未来FPGA将进一步模块化，不同功能（逻辑、DSP、AI、I/O）分别用最适合的制程制造Chiplet architecture: VP1902 already uses 4-chiplet design. Future FPGAs will further modularize logic, DSP, AI, and I/O across optimal process nodes
  • AI集成加深：Versal ACAP的AI Engine已证明FPGA+AI的可行性。下一代将集成更多专用AI加速单元Deeper AI integration: Versal ACAP's AI Engine proved FPGA+AI viability. Next-gen will integrate more dedicated AI accelerator blocks
  • RISC-V替代Arm：Lattice和Microchip已采用RISC-V硬核。开源ISA降低授权成本，对军工供应安全也有好处RISC-V replacing Arm: Lattice and Microchip already use RISC-V hard cores. Open ISA reduces licensing cost and improves defense supply security
  • eFPGA IP：QuickLogic等公司提供可嵌入ASIC的FPGA IP核，实现ASIC量产 + FPGA灵活性的结合eFPGA IP: QuickLogic and others offer FPGA IP blocks embeddable into ASICs, combining ASIC volume economics with FPGA flexibility
  • 制程追赶：AMD的Versal Gen 2转向N6是第一步，业界预计2028-2030年高端FPGA将进入5nm/3nm时代Process catch-up: AMD's Versal Gen 2 moving to N6 is step one; industry expects high-end FPGAs to reach 5nm/3nm by 2028-2030
  • 国产替代：中国高云半导体（Gowin）、紫光同创、安路科技等在28nm以上节点追赶，但高端受制裁限制，与AMD/Altera差距仍在2-3代China domestic: Gowin, UNISOC FPGA, Anlogic etc. competing at 28nm+ nodes, but high-end constrained by sanctions — still 2-3 generations behind AMD/Altera
